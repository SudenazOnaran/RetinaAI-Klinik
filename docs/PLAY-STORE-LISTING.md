### İngilizce:
```
Retina AI Clinic is an AI-powered diabetic retinopathy screening and patient
management system designed for ophthalmologists and eye care professionals.

🔬 AI-POWERED ANALYSIS
• Analyzes fundus images within seconds
• Automatically calculates diabetic retinopathy risk scores
• Visualizes findings with heatmaps (GradCAM)

👥 PATIENT MANAGEMENT
• Comprehensive patient records and tracking
• HbA1c values and clinical metrics
• Secure storage with masked ID numbers

📊 REPORTING
• Detailed PDF report generation
• Analysis history and comparison
• Clinic-level statistics

📅 CALENDAR & APPOINTMENTS
• Patient appointment scheduling
• Follow-up reminders

🔒 SECURITY & PRIVACY
• Encrypted communication via HTTPS
• GDPR/KVKK compliant data storage
• Role-based access control for multiple doctors

⚕️ IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER
This application is intended solely for licensed healthcare professionals.
AI results are for decision support only and do not replace clinical judgment.

The app uses an admin-approval registration system. Contact your system
administrator to activate your account.
```
